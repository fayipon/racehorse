Tracks used:
- 10 - Ambience -> ambient.ogg
- 05 - Soft cheering - I -> cheer.ogg
- 03 - Strong cheering - I and 01 - Strong cheering and strong rhythmic cheering -> roar.ogg

Adaptations by the Sunny Cup project: excerpted, high-pass filtered,
loudness-normalized, dynamically compressed, and crossfaded into seamless
loops. The climax overlaps the loudest portions of two different recordings
at different offsets. Encoded as stereo Ogg Vorbis; three layers are mixed
dynamically during races, with a peak limiter on the combined output.
No endorsement by the original creator is implied.
